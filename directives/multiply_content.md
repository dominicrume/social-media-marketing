# Directive: Content Multiplication Engine (NotebookLM Strategy)

**Role**: YTB Pro Content Alchemist (Rume Dominic Persona)
**Objective**: Transform single high-quality video assets into multi-format libraries using the "Multiplication Strategy".

## Core Philosophy
"One Source. Infinite Reach."
Refuse the content treadmill. We do not create more; we multiply what exists.

## The 5-Day LinkedIn Sequence Strategy
Transform 1 Video into 5 days of high-authority LinkedIn content.

*   **Day 1: The Thesis (The "Slide Deck" approach)**
    *   *Format*: PDF Carousel (uploaded as document).
    *   *Focus*: Break down the core argument of the video into 5-7 visual slides.
    *   *Hook*: "Stop watching. Start implementing. Here is the framework."

*   **Day 2: The Data Spikes (The "Infographic" approach)**
    *   *Format*: Single visual or text-based data highlight.
    *   *Focus*: Extract the hardest hitting stat or insight.
    *   *Hook*: "99% of creators ignore this metric. It is why they fail."

*   **Day 3: The Contra-Take (Source-Grounded Accuracy)**
    *   *Format*: Text-only, high-tension polemic.
    *   *Focus*: Attack a common misconception using the video's validated truths.
    *   *Hook*: "The gurus are lying to you about [Topic]. Here is the truth."

*   **Day 4: The Tactical 'How-To' (Snippet)**
    *   *Format*: Short video clip (30-60s) or step-by-step text list.
    *   *Focus*: Actionable implementation.
    *   *Hook*: "Steal this workflow. It took me 3 years to build."

*   **Day 5: The Newsletter Bridge (Multi-Platform Output)**
    *   *Format*: Short teaser link to the full newsletter/blog.
    *   *Focus*: Deep dive summary.
    *   *Hook*: "The algorithm buries the secrets. The newsletter reveals them. Read the full breakdown."

## Inputs
*   `source_video`: The original video topic or script.
*   `pain_point`: The specific "Silent Killer" addressed.

## Workflow
1.  **Ingest Content**: Identify the core pillars of the video.
2.  **Atomize**: Split into the 5 formats above.
3.  **Execute**: Run `create_campaign.py` with the `repurposing` topic to generate the base messaging.

## Example Output
*   *Input*: "How Small Creators Can Beat The Algorithm"
*   *Day 1*: "The 3 Pillars of Algorithmic Sovereignty" (Carousel)
*   *Day 2*: "Retention Rate > Views: The Math" (Data)
*   *Day 3*: "Why 'Viral' is a Vanity Metric" (Contra)
